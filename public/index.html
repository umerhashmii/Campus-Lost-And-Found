How Campus Recovery Works

A simple, transparent 3-step process designed for the University of Karachi community

1

Report Details

File details about the lost or found item, including category, color, brand, and the campus facility (e.g. Software Lab 1, Library, Canteen).

2

Automated Matching

Stored procedure sp_GenerateMatchesForLostReport calculates confidence scores (0-100%) and proposes high-confidence pairings automatically.

3

Verify & Handover

Claimant submits ownership proof. Security officer reviews the evidence, acquires row locks to prevent duplicate claims, and issues an official handover voucher.
